## Verbatim text (as rendered at retrieval)

> § 14-548. /SIX, Sixth District Overlay District.
>
> (1) Applicability. The Sixth District Overlay District applies to lots located within District No. 6, as defined in Section 20-501 (Boundaries of Districts).
>
> (2) The following regulations apply within the /SIX Overlay District:
>   (.a) Medical, Dental, Health Practitioner uses, as an accessory use, shall be located in an attached building.
>   (.b) Multiple principal uses are permitted in single structure.
>   (.c) Accessory dwelling units shall not be permitted.
>
> (3) Development Standards.
>   (.1) In the RSA-5 zoning district, maximum height shall be equal to the height of the tallest abutting building or 35 feet, whichever is less.
>   (.2) In the RSA-5 zoning district, the minimum lot area shall be 1,440 sq. ft.
>   (.3) The minimum lot area shall be 1,440 sq. ft., except as provided in § 14-701(2)(b)[1].
>   (.4) In the CMX-2 zoning district, the number of dwelling units in a structure may not exceed one per 500 sq. ft. of lot area.
>   (.5) In the I-1 and I-2 zoning districts, the maximum occupied area is 80%.
>   (.6) In the I-1 zoning district, the maximum floor area ratio is 3.0.
>   (.7) In the I-2 zoning district, the maximum floor area ratio is 5.0.
>
> (4) Areas 1, 2, and 3 (described by the boundary text) — prohibited uses include Amusement Arcades, Pool or Billiard Rooms, Adult Entertainment, Check Cashing, Pawn Shops, Tatoo [sic] Parlors, and Pay-Day Loan establishments; applicable in the I-1, I-2, and I-3 zoning districts.
